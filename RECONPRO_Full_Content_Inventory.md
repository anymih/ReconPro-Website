## 10. RESOURCE LIBRARIES (/resource-libraries + sub-pages)
**Archive H1:** "Resource Library Archives." Sub: "Curated references to help you validate regulations, scope, and feasibility before you build."

**Sample sub-page — Delhi Building Bye Laws** (City Wise Building Bye Laws, 5 min read):
- Governing body: MCD (Municipal Corporation of Delhi).
- **FAR Exclusions:** lift machine rooms, open shafts/fire staircases, mumty over staircases (max 3m), pergolas (up to 20% ground coverage), basements (parking/storage).
- **Ground Coverage Exclusions:** balconies, canopies, porches, voids, cladding up to 150mm.
- **FAR & Ground Coverage Table (MPD 2021):** Plot up to 50 sqm → 90% coverage, FAR 350, 3 DUs. 50-100 sqm → 90%, 350, 4 DUs. 100-250 sqm → 75%, 300, 4 DUs. 250-750 sqm → 75%, 225, 6 DUs. Above 750 sqm → 50%, 200, 9-21 DUs.
- **Setbacks:** Below 100 sqm → 0/0/0/0m. 100-250 → 3/0/0/0m. 250-500 → 3/3/3/0m. Above 500 → 6/3/3/3m (front/rear/side1/side2).
- **Max building height:** 17.5m for individual residential plots.
- **Height exemptions:** roof tanks (1.8m), mumty (3m), solar panels, lift machine rooms.
- **General requirements:** drainage standards, min wall thickness 230mm, balcony max width 1.5m (FAR-free), parapet wall max 1.5m (low-rise)/1.8m (high-rise), boundary wall max 1.5m solid + 1.5m perforated, basement min height 2.4m, stilt parking 2.4-2.7m height, lift mandatory above 15m height, staircase min tread 25cm/max riser 19cm, courtyard min 3m×3m for light/ventilation, bathroom ventilation shaft min 1.5-3 sqm.
