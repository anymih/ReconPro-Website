OUR METHODOLOGY

ReconPro’s 4-Step Process to Home Construction

A structured, logical advisory flow ensuring complete clarity from choosing the right build path to final handover.

01

Approach Selection

We evaluate your plot, budget, and goals to recommend the right path: Turnkey Builder, Collaborator, Architect + Contractor, or DIY.

02

Agency Evaluation

Deep-dive audit of agency fitment across project management tools, material specs, financial legal due diligence, and delivered project surveys.

03

Agreement & Specs

Drafting PM guidelines, redrafting one-sided contract terms, tweaking building specs to safety codes, and contract negotiation.

04

Project Management

Regular site audits, workmanship critiques, and ReBOS mobile app tracking until final project handover.
